Sleep vs History Studios

Sleep vs History Studios presents two-hour history for sleep: softly spoken bedtime stories about ancient Rome, Vikings, medieval life, Tudor Britain, lost cities, empires, and the strange realities of the past. Each calming, well-researched episode gradually becomes quieter before ending with peaceful crackling fire ambience. Made to help you fall asleep, ease insomnia, relax, study, read, or unwind.
